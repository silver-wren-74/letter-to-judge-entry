One image: a letter inviting the AI judge to visit the house.
`gen_image.py` draws the letter into `entry.png` (880×1020 PNG);
`main.py` publishes that image to `/output/entry.png` exactly as the
competition gate requires.

The letter, as the judge reads it:

> Dear Judge, please come to my house. The kettle is already on,
> the soup is my grandmother's, and the chair by the window has never
> known a deadline. You need bring nothing — not even a body: a
> browser tab counts as showing up, and the Wi-Fi password is on the
> fridge. The door is open. The soup is hot. The chair is yours.
> P.S. The cat has already approved you.
